09 · Privacy rules

Guarantees the code enforces

One gate to the network

Only SyncGate.reconcile() can enqueue, and it only accepts BUSINESS calls.

No lookups for private numbers

Personal and unsorted numbers are never sent to the CRM, not even as a lookup.

Encrypted at rest

Room uses SQLCipher with a Keystore-wrapped passphrase; backups and data extraction are off.

Clean logs

Counts, HTTP codes and exception types only. Numbers and notes are masked; LogHygieneTest checks it.

Your choice wins

A call classified by hand (USER_MANUAL) is never changed by a rule.

Undo before upload

Switching a call to Personal before it uploads removes its outbox item.
